BLOODSWORN — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-16 · Effective: 2026-08-16

Summary: this game collects no personal data.
BLOODSWORN is a fully offline, single-device game. There is no server, no account, and no advertising or analytics software. Everything the app writes stays on the user's device and is never transmitted anywhere.

1. Personal data we collect

None. The app does not collect, request, or transmit:

  • Name, email address, phone number, postal address, or any other identifier
  • Account credentials (the app has no sign-up or login at all)
  • Device identifiers (advertising ID, IDFA, IDFV, Android ID, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files on the device
  • Usage logs, access logs, cookies, crash reports, or analytics events

2. Game data stored on your device only

To let you continue your progress, the app writes the following exclusively to storage on your own device. This is not personal data, and no one — including the developer — can access it.

Storage locationContents
localStorage
(in-app WebView)		 Game progress: gold, sanctum upgrade levels, unlocks, settings (volume, controls), and play statistics.
Storage key: bloodsworn.save.v1
OS app-private storage
(Android SharedPreferences / iOS UserDefaults, via @capacitor/preferences)

The same value is written to two places for a purely technical reason: on iOS the WebView's localStorage may be cleared by the operating system under storage pressure, so a copy is kept in OS storage to avoid losing progress. Both locations are on-device and are never transmitted over a network.

How to delete it: use the reset option in the in-game settings screen, or uninstall the app. Either action removes this data from the device completely. There is no deletion request process, because no copy exists anywhere else.

3. Sharing with third parties

None. Since nothing is collected, there is nothing to share, sell, or process on our behalf. The app contains no ad networks, analytics SDKs, crash reporting tools, social logins, or in-app purchases.

4. Network communication

The app does not connect to the internet in order to play. All game assets are bundled inside the installed application.

The Android build declares the INTERNET permission. This is required because the app's screen is served from a device-local address (https://localhost); it is not used to contact any external server. No other permissions are requested.

5. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (recommended age: 13+) and collects no personal information, including age.

6. Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or retained, there is no data to access, correct, delete, or restrict. Game data stored on your device can be deleted by you at any time (see section 2).

7. Changes to this policy

Any change will be posted on this page together with its effective date. If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released.
